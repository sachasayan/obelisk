Obelisk 3D room asset credits

Furniture and rug

Models by Amazon.com from the Amazon Berkeley Objects dataset, licensed under CC BY 4.0. Obelisk uses the optimized glTF copies by CodePawl and adjusts their scale and placement. The lamp emission is set in the simulator.

  • Rivet Revolve upholstered sofa (B07PBZ2PSS)
  • Stone & Beam Deco floor lamp (B07376VCCW)
  • Stone & Beam terracotta table lamp (B07ML7J57M)
  • Stone & Beam Culver side table (B075Z8FV7N)
  • Stone & Beam industrial coffee table (B07QF9Y71V)
  • Stone & Beam ombré stoneware vase (B07B8NVHX1)
  • Rivet criss-cross woven wool rug (B0735SLBYD)

The dataset was assembled by Matthieu Guillaumin, Thomas Dideriksen, Kenan Deng, Himanshu Arora, Arnab Dhua, Xi (Brian) Zhang, and Tomas Yago-Vicente at Amazon.com, and Jasmine Collins, Shubham Goel, and Jitendra Malik at UC Berkeley. Dataset attribution and research.

Carpet and lighting

  • Carpet 001 by ambientCG, CC0. Obelisk uses the color and OpenGL normal maps, converted to WebP.
  • Studio Small 09 by Sergej Majboroda for Poly Haven, CC0. The 1K HDRI provides indirect lighting and reflections.
