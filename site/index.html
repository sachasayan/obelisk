Visuals and games, one remote.

Leave a clock or a quiet starfield on the wall. Switch to a game when you want to play.

Visuals

Pick a clock face and color, tune Pulse, check transit arrivals, or put a twinkling sky or an animated sunset on the wall.

Games

Drag the Munchman D-pad, move a paddle in Pong, or watch an autoplay round of Lightcycles.

Your phone is the controller.

Open Obelisk in a browser on the same trusted network as the installation. Select a mode, play with touch controls, or turn the display off from the remote.

  • Hold and slide across the Munchman D-pad to change direction.
  • Use arrow keys or WASD when you’re at a keyboard.
  • Keep the same remote beside the 2D or 3D simulator.

Questions

Do I need LED panels to try it?

No. The simulator runs the modes locally and shows the output as individual pixels or as a mounted 3D installation.

Can I use a different shape or number of panels?

Yes. Set the tile geometry and the horizontal and vertical tile counts. The simulator is a way to check how modes fit before you wire the physical setup.

Does this website control my display?

The remote is served by your own Obelisk instance. Use that instance’s address on your local network to control the panel.

Can I write my own mode?

Yes. Obelisk is MIT-licensed. The built-in modes share a panel drawing interface and are a starting point for your own visuals or games. Browse the mode source.
